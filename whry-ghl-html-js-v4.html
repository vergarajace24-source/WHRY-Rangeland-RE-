Our Programs

Legendary shows and timeless hits

Goddard's Gold

Sundays — 9:00am - 12:00pm

Goddard's Gold is three fresh music filled hours of hits from the 60's and 70's hosted by the legendary Steve Goddard. Every Sunday morning from 9:00am - 12:00pm, Steve opens the vault on another spotlight artist complete with their music and story. Steve knows everyone and everything when it comes to great Oldies!

Unshackled!

Sundays — 7:30am - 8:00am

Real people...real life stories...stirring, dramatic accounts of hopelessness, and the hope that changes everything. "UNSHACKLED!" the award-winning radio drama from Pacific Garden Mission in Chicago, grips the heart with compelling and relevant stories of transformed lives.

Wolfman Jack

Sundays — 1:00pm - 6:00pm

Every night for over six decades, millions of people have listened to Wolfman Jack on the radio. They love his outrageous persona and the great music he plays on his show.

Throwback Nation Radio

Saturdays — 6:00pm - 11:00pm

Every Saturday night, Tony Lorino brings back that nostalgic feeling with Throwback Nation Radio. Spinning the songs you love to play from back in the day, his energetic, upbeat, and NEVER sleepy, Throwback Nation Radio can be heard Saturdays from 6:00pm - 11:00pm.

How much does it cost to list my home with Rangeland Real Estate?

Listing commissions are negotiable and discussed upfront during your free consultation. There are no hidden fees, and you will know exactly what you pay before you sign anything.

Do you work with first-time home buyers?

Yes. We walk first-time buyers through every step, from pre-approval to closing, with no pressure and no rush. Most of our Ironwood buyers are first-timers or relocating from out of state.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Ironwood, Bessemer, Wakefield, Ramsay, Bergland, and the surrounding Gogebic County area, plus Hurley and northern Wisconsin across the state line.

Can you help me buy land or hunting property?

Absolutely. Land and recreational property is our specialty. We know the back roads, lake access points, and hunting ground across the western UP and northern Wisconsin.
